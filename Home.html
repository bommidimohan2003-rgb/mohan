Top Indian Cities to Visit

Delhi

The capital of India, home to India Gate, Red Fort, Qutub Minar and rich heritage.

Mumbai

The city of dreams, famous for Bollywood, Gateway of India, and Marine Drive.

Jaipur

The Pink City, known for Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort, and vibrant Rajasthani culture.

Varanasi

The spiritual capital of India, famous for Ganga Aarti, temples, and ghats.
